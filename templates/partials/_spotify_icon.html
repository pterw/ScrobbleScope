{# The official Spotify icon (F-B21-60), as Spotify supplies it in 2024-spotify-logo-icon.zip from developer.spotify.com; the files under static/images/brand/spotify/ are byte-for-byte copies, never redrawn or recoloured. Spotify's design guidelines allow the green icon only on a pure black or white background, otherwise black on light and white on dark. The app's surfaces are neither pure white (light theme) nor pure black (dark theme), so the theme swaps between the two monochrome files; CSS in results.css shows the one that matches data-theme (under forced colours, the one that matches the system palette, because the saved theme may disagree with it and the wrong file would vanish). The width and height attributes are the file's own viewBox in whole pixels: they only reserve the box's aspect ratio (CSS sets the 24px height), so the attribution line does not shift when the SVG arrives. The alt text is empty because every caller names Spotify in words next to it (a link's aria-label, or visible text). Sizing (24px tall, over the 21px minimum) and clear space live on `.spotify-icon` and its wrappers. #}
